Five-Stage Pipeline

Every image passes through these stages in order.

01
Denoising
Non-Local Means removes film grain and sensor noise.
02
Scratch Detection
Morphological top-hat at H, V, and diagonal orientations with aspect-ratio and density filtering.
03
Inpainting
Navier-Stokes fills in detected damage, then a light NLM pass blends the seams.
04
Enhancement
CLAHE (clip 2.0, tile 8×8) boosts local contrast without over-exposing bright areas.
05
Color Correction
Auto-detects sepia, grayscale, or color and applies the right fix.
